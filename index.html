    How this symptom checker works

    This tool doesn't try to guess a specific medical condition — that requires a real clinical exam and testing, and the same symptoms can point to many different causes. Instead, it does something more reliable: it checks your selected symptoms, age, sex, and (if relevant) pregnancy status against well-established red-flag patterns used in medical triage (like the FAST signs of stroke, or classic heart attack warning combinations) and gives you a clear urgency level so you know how quickly to act.

    Tap a popular symptom chip, type to search and add any symptom, or open "Browse full list by body area" for the complete checklist. You'll get one of four urgency levels: Emergency, Urgent Care, See a Doctor Soon, or Self-Care.

    Urgency level reference

    Emergency
    Call emergency services or go to the ER immediately. These symptom patterns can indicate a life-threatening condition.
    Urgent Care
    See a doctor or urgent care clinic today. Not immediately life-threatening, but shouldn't wait days.
    See a Doctor Soon
    Schedule an appointment within the next few days, especially if symptoms persist or worsen.
    Self-Care
    Mild, common symptoms that often resolve with rest and home care — but monitor for change.
